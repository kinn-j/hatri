# Hatri rule book

## Pantry ingredient classification

Keep the existing category rail, subcategory pills, photographic ingredient tiles, spacing, and selection behavior. Organize ingredient IDs under the grocery-style categories Vegetables & Fruits; Atta, Rice & Dal; Oil, Ghee & Masala; Dairy & Bread; Dry Fruits & Cereals; Sauces & Spreads; Bakery & Snacks; Drinks & Specialty; and Meat & Fish (visible only with Veg Mode off). In non-vegetarian mode, dairy may be labeled Dairy, Bread & Eggs. Eggs and animal-derived meat, fish and stock must never appear in the vegetarian pantry selector, including its drawer or search results.

The Vegetables & Fruits category has exactly four subcategory views, in this order: Fresh Vegetables, Fresh Fruits, Coriander & Others, and Exotics. Do not add a separate Seasonal view: seasonal vegetables are already in Fresh Vegetables, and seasonal fruits in Fresh Fruits. Do not restore the older roots/gourds/leafy/peas subdivisions, Freshly Cut & Sprouts, Trusted Organics, or Flowers & Leaves. Every produce ID must appear in exactly one view. Arrange related produce next to each other within their subcategory; onion, pearl onion and spring onion must be consecutive.

Reuse the same existing subcategory-pill layout for Atta, Rice & Dal (Rice & Poha; Dal & Pulses; Atta & Flours; Grains & Staples; Noodles & Batters), Oil, Ghee & Masala (Oil & Ghee; Ground Masala; Khada Masala; Kitchen Basics), and Dairy & Bread (Milk & Dairy; Paneer & Cheese; Bread & Flatbreads; Plant Proteins; Eggs). Eggs is shown only with Veg Mode off. Every ingredient in these categories belongs to exactly one relevant subcategory, with no omitted items. Changing main categories starts on the first subcategory. Select all and Clear all act only on the visible subcategory, preserving photo tiles and scroll position where possible. Other main categories retain their existing presentation.

Prefer raw, purchasable ingredients over cooked preparation states. For example, boiled potato is potato and cooked bajra roti must be made from bajra flour with the cooking and drying/resting directions in its recipe. Do not silently equate dry beans to ready-to-eat beans in genuine no-cook recipes; use a clearly labeled shelf-stable ready-to-eat product where appropriate or require cooking and revise the timing/category. Whenever ingredient IDs change, update English and Hindi instructions, measured quantities, the recipe spreadsheet and regression coverage together.

## Whole meals

Build whole meals in descending tiers: first **six dishes** (two mains, bread, rice, curd and sweet), then **five dishes** (two mains, bread, curd and sweet, omitting rice), then **four dishes** (two mains, bread and sweet, omitting rice and curd). Only enter the next tier after exhausting feasible meals in the previous tier using the remaining recipes. Two mains, one bread and one sweet are never optional. All dishes inside a meal must be distinct. Across one set of whole-meal suggestions, each dish may appear only once, including any optional salad. The rule covers all result groups and the featured meal shown alongside the list.

Use the same recipe identity across categories and languages. Reserve dishes only when a complete meal meets the active filters. When a required course runs out of eligible dishes, continue with the smaller tiers instead of stopping all meals. With the full catalog and default filters, offer at least 25 themed six-dish meals, followed by as many feasible five- and four-dish meals as possible. Pair two mains from the same region or meaningful everyday cuisine grouping and prefer local accompaniments. Savory rice-based one-pot recipes can fill the rice course, and yogurt-based dishes such as kadhi and chaas can fill the curd course.

Do not cap the meal generator or the rendered meal list at four, fifty, or any other number. Try alternative accompaniments when a first pairing cannot complete the applicable tier, without repeating dishes anywhere in the suggestion set. The **ready to cook** meal subsection includes only meals with zero missing pantry ingredients; it is not the entire collection. Always display the total number of complete meal sets, the six-/five-/four-dish breakdown, and ready versus shopping counts. List six-dish sets first, then five-dish sets, then four-dish sets, while retaining pantry-readiness grouping within each tier. Respect explicitly selected region, time, diet and Hostel filters, and indicate when a region filter narrows the meal list.

Time filters count soaking that cannot overlap ordinary prep. Whole-meal estimates allow soaking for separate dishes to happen in parallel.

Tapping any four-, five- or six-dish meal suggestion opens a separate, accessible meal-details tray/dialog, never an inline expansion that shifts the surrounding suggestion cards. The tray includes all member dishes and existing shopping-cart and Table actions; individual dish tiles can still open their complete recipe detail. A small close button and Escape close the tray, returning focus and scroll position to the original meal card. Tapping Add to Table on the meal card must not open the tray. Remove the **Decide for me** dice and its featured pick entirely; the main meal list includes every generated meal.

Optimize the **total number of non-repeating meals first**, with six-dish meals preferred when counts tie, and pantry convenience afterward. Consider every eligible accompaniment instead of truncating each course to a small top-N shortlist. Retry alternative allocations and reserve recipes only after a complete match; optional salads must never reduce the number of complete meals. Keep international accompaniments with compatible cuisines and group generic Indian dal/sabzi dishes into meaningful everyday meals rather than mislabeling them as international. Audit unique eligible mains, breads, rice, curd dishes and sweets whenever meal counts seem unexpectedly low. The final four-dish availability upper bound is the minimum of half the main count (rounded down), the bread count and the sweet count; regional and time compatibility can reduce the actual maximum. Never inflate meal counts by repeating dishes or treating sweet rice puddings, millet dishes or unrelated cuisines as an ordinary rice accompaniment.

## Complete suggestions and diet categories

Never impose a maximum on eligible individual dishes, search matches, complete six-dish meal sets, or cart suggestions. All complete meal sets must remain available without cutting off at 50; all cart suggestions must remain available without cutting off at 100. Dish and search cards may progressively render in batches for mobile performance, but must automatically load the rest as the user scrolls and provide a **Show all remaining dishes** fallback. Show the full match count and never silently omit a dish because it already appears inside a suggested six-dish meal or featured suggestion.

Every unique dish must belong to at least one valid **dish-type** category in the carousel **and** exactly one internal diet classification: Vegetarian or Non-vegetarian. Eggs, meat, fish, poultry, seafood, animal gelatin and meat stock are non-vegetarian, including optional ingredients; "eggless" recipes without animal ingredients remain vegetarian. **Never show Vegetarian or Non-vegetarian as carousel categories.** Diet is controlled solely by the top-screen Veg Mode switch: on shows every eligible vegetarian dish (1,092 of 1,152 catalog dishes as of 27 November 2026); off shows both vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, including all 60 non-vegetarian dishes. Every dish in either mode must remain discoverable under at least one of the existing dish-type categories and under All dishes; a dish may belong to multiple types. Choosing any dish-type category replaces the initial default Whole meal selection; users may subsequently combine category cards explicitly. Neither categories nor modes impose numeric caps.

Hostel recipes must also belong to the same ordinary dish-type categories as all other recipes and retain their internal vegetarian/non-vegetarian classifications. Hostel Mode is an additional equipment filter; switching it off restores the same Hostel recipes to the ordinary categories, not a separate exclusive catalog. Category-card counts represent all catalog entries available in the current Veg/Hostel Mode, without limiting the count by time, region, occasion, or the number initially rendered. Choosing **All dishes** must show the complete catalog for the current Veg/Hostel Mode, regardless of saved time, region, and occasion filters. Keep every match discoverable as users scroll even when it exceeds 100 recipes.

On the Cook tab, show a Back to top button as soon as the user enters the **second screen of Suggestions** (one viewport height below the Suggestions heading). Hide it during the first screen and outside the results. Activating it returns to the top of Cook.

## My Table cooking shortlist

Every individual recipe and four-/five-/six-dish meal suggestion must offer an **Add to Table** action independently of the shopping cart, including ready-to-cook items. A selected meal is saved as one complete, themed set with four, five or six distinct recipe identities. A user may also add an individual recipe, even if the same recipe is part of a selected meal. Repeatedly choosing the same item toggles its selection rather than creating duplicates. The Table page must show all saved selections together, allow opening recipes and removing individual entries, and never change cart ingredients or pantry selections. Save the shortlist locally and through existing signed-in state synchronization; clear it on account switch or sign-out with the other user-specific data.

The order of dishes does not change a saved meal's identity. Independent preference changes should survive edits from another tab or device. Backup codes include the current planner filters and accept older pantry and cart backups.

## Bottom navigation

The bottom navigation always has exactly four destinations, in order: Cook, Reels, Shopping list and Table. Never include Kitchen in this bar; My Kitchen and its backup/account tools remain available from Profile and settings. Use the same compact dark, rounded floating navigator in light and dark themes, with horizontal vector icons and labels, a separate charcoal active pill, and a green active icon. Keep tap targets, text contrast, safe-area spacing, screen-reader names, and active-page indication usable on narrow phones. Reels retains the shared navigator; shopping-list overlays may temporarily cover it without losing the selected destination.

## Recipe filters

Recipe cards, filters, and the recipe catalog omit heaviness ratings. Ignore saved heaviness selections when loading preferences and omit them when saving preferences.

Keep **Time I have (prep + cook)** inside the **More filters** sheet, alongside Region and Occasion, not as a separate slider on the main Cook page. Keep all six existing time ranges and their multi-select semantics. Selections are draft-only until Apply; closing without applying discards edits, Reset filters restores unrestricted time/region/occasion, and the More filters badge includes Time when restricted. Preserve saved time filters and recipe matching across reloads.

## Adding recipes and ingredients

For future recipe additions, generate or source a **dedicated, accurate photo file for every new dish**, not a photo borrowed from a different dish. Every new ingredient gets a separate recognizable ingredient photo; audit existing pantry IDs without image mappings and close those gaps in the same change. Store the image files in the repository, wire the first-render image paths and update the Excel image fields before opening a PR.

Every request to add a dish means a complete catalog entry: stable English dish ID, appropriate region and dish-type category, vegetarian/non-vegetarian classification, accurate pantry ingredient IDs, measured ingredient quantities, tested cooking directions, an image that depicts the dish, a packaged Hindi dish name/ingredient lines/cooking method, and a matching dated row in `docs/Hatri_Recipe_Catalog.xlsx` on both the dish and recipe tabs. New ingredient IDs must be included in the pantry selector with an actual ingredient image and Hindi name; prefer an existing canonical ID when the ingredient is already represented. Validate uniqueness against the full catalog and verify all referenced image paths and offline Hindi coverage before raising a PR. Do not substitute an unrelated dish picture merely to fill a photo slot. Reusing an existing photo is acceptable only where it accurately depicts the same food or a visually equivalent presentation; clearly disclose photo reuse when reporting the addition.

## Language


Author app text and recipe content in English and retain stable recipe and ingredient IDs across all languages. All published recipe names, measured ingredient lines, and cooking steps must have a packaged Hindi translation in `hindi-catalog.js` before release. App navigation, accessibility labels, regions, filters, ingredient names, and recipe text must work in Hindi with Google Translate completely unavailable. The Hindi pack must be loaded and cached with the app shell; do not use a network call for catalog content. When source instructions change, regenerate and review their translations before merging, with automated coverage tests to identify missing source text. Machine translations are drafts: review numerical quantities, temperature, timing, negation and food-safety terminology. Never silently replace a missing or unsafe method with an invented Hindi instruction. Keep account identities and form entries verbatim. Hindi search uses packaged and cached catalog terms and keeps the entered query on the device. Reserve optional runtime translation for uncataloged or user-generated content, with failure cooldown.

Mark any untranslated instructions as English and identify the fallback to the reader. Voice search is offered only when speech recognition is available on-device for the chosen language. Community video captions and credits remain verbatim; legacy video titles are retained only for compatibility and are not displayed.

## Offline app shell

Every local script required to restore saved pantry, cart, Table and recipe state must be precached in the service worker shell, including `preferences.js`. Treat HTML, JavaScript and the manifest as one atomic, versioned offline shell: install every required asset before activation, bypass stale HTTP-cache responses during installation, and serve these shell assets cache-first. Do not overwrite individual shell assets from network responses after installation, which could mix release versions. Derive the shell request allowlist from the precache list and advance the cache version whenever a shell asset changes. Leave saved state in local storage untouched across worker upgrades. Test that saved pantry, cart and Table selections survive installation over an older release and repeated offline reloads in both Chromium and WebKit.

## Community recipe videos

Community recipe videos are public once published. A signed-in user may upload an MP4, WebM, or MOV video up to 100 MB, with the binary stored in Firebase Storage and only its metadata stored in Firestore. Anonymous users may browse public videos but may not upload, edit, or delete posts. The feed shows one video per viewport, advances by vertical scroll, and loads at most 30 newest posts per initial query.

Each new video must select an exact, existing Hatri recipe by its stable English catalog name and publish that `recipeName` in the public video metadata; editing a video can update this link. Display a photographic tile linked to that recipe on each reel, opening the existing complete recipe tray (image, ingredients, method, Hindi support and actions). Closing the tray restores the current reel's scroll position and playback. Older posts without a `recipeName` may link only by an exact existing catalog title; never guess an unrelated dish. If no exact match exists, identify the missing link and let the creator attach a recipe through Edit. Reels must retain the same four bottom destinations as other views, with a direct return to Suggestions; Kitchen remains reachable from Profile on Cook.

The Reels back control is an arrow with an accessible label, not a separate text button to Suggestions. A cook creates a reel in a gallery-style two-step screen: choose a local video and preview it, then write a caption and use a compact recipe-search action to map the exact Hatri recipe. Keep optional timed-caption configuration under More options. Neither feed cards nor creation/edit forms display a video-title field; an internal title may be derived from the linked recipe for backend compatibility with existing records. Every search input across the app, including recipe, ingredient, region and reel-to-recipe searches, has an explicit one-tap clear button that triggers the same live filtering as manually deleting the query.

Keep drafts and staging files private. The backend validates the declared size, format, and uploaded file header before publication. Cancel stops an upload before publication. A cook can start up to five uploads per UTC day, with at least 30 seconds between starts. A credit is optional and defaults to Hatri cook. Published records exclude account identifiers. Viewers can open a direct post link, use native playback controls and creator-provided captions, and signed-in users can report a post. Creators can edit their metadata and remove their posts.

## Activity sharing

Activity sharing is opt-in per browser and account. The server checks consent, deduplicates named events, and limits recorded events per account. Typed and dictated recipe queries are excluded. Activity records expire after 90 days.
